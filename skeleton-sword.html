Watch for: skeletons THROW their swords (evolvable SwordThrow gene) — skilled throwers lead your movement, so fake them out; a thrower must fetch its sword and takes +50% damage while unarmed. Parry and Riposte are separate genes now: parry decides whether they block; riposte decides whether they answer — and a skel that parries your bolt from across the field will usually hold, because you're out of range. Evolution is a fitness-weighted roulette — hits, damage, parries, ripostes and reactions count most, survival is capped. Parry rates are running high and are deliberately NOT capped — no-limit evolution is still being tested. Size & speed are unbounded.
